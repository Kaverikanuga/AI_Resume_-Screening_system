AI Resume Screening System — Final Year Engineering Project

Project Overview

The AI Resume Screening System is a full-stack, production-grade SaaS platform that uses artificial intelligence and natural language processing to analyze resumes, evaluate ATS (Applicant Tracking System) compatibility, match candidates to job descriptions, optimize LinkedIn and Naukri profiles, and provide AI-powered career guidance.

Key Features
  • AI-powered resume parsing & analysis
  • ATS score & resume health assessment
  • Job description matching
  • LinkedIn & Naukri profile optimization
  • AI career assistant chatbot
  • Resume builder with 5 templates
  • Professional PDF reports
  • Admin panel & analytics
Tech Stack
Flask SQLAlchemy SQLite Flask-Login Flask-WTF PyPDF2 pdfplumber spaCy NLTK scikit-learn pandas ReportLab Bootstrap 5 Chart.js AOS Font Awesome
Architecture

Model-View-Controller (MVC) pattern with modular Flask blueprints. The AI engine is separated into parser, extractor, scorer, and optimizer modules for clean, reusable architecture. Redis-style service layer abstracts business logic.
